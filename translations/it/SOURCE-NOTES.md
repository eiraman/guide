# Notes on the source

Points of the English source, [EIRAMAN-GUIDE.md](../../EIRAMAN-GUIDE.md) 1.0.0-rc.7 (commit `47b3021`), found
ambiguous or odd while preparing the Italian translation, [EIRAMAN-GUIDE.it.md](EIRAMAN-GUIDE.it.md). Each
point is translated faithfully and is not corrected: the English text is canonical, and a correction of the
source needs an RFC. These notes are part of the translation and share its version and its
[CHANGELOG](CHANGELOG.md). They are not normative.

| # | Section | Source text | Point | Italian rendering |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | §0 Purpose, Normative language; §15 Conformance | "what makes an application conformant"; "An application conforms" | "application" can mean a software application or an application of the method | «applicazione», which keeps both readings |
| 2 | §0 Normative language | "SHOULD NOT" | The key word is defined, but no rule of the Guide uses it: its two occurrences are in the definition | «NON DOVREBBE», in the definition only |
| 3 | §4 Human Manager | "MUST receive technical decisions translated into …" | The obligation is placed on the Human Manager, who receives, not on who delivers | «DEVE ricevere le decisioni tecniche tradotte in …» |
| 4 | §4 Liability towards third parties | "in the informative mode the owner exercises this responsibility through mandate and acceptance. The owner exercises this responsibility through mandate, class and acceptance" | Two nearly identical sentences, the special case before the general rule; the second does not say that it holds in the blocking mode | Both sentences kept, in the same order: «nella modalità informativa il titolare esercita questa responsabilità attraverso mandato e accettazione. Il titolare esercita questa responsabilità attraverso mandato, classe e accettazione» |
| 5 | §5 Deny | "unless a requirement of level 1 that the paragraph makes due is due and not met" | Convoluted: "due" twice, and "the paragraph" refers to a paragraph of §14.2 | «a meno che un requisito del livello 1 che quel paragrafo rende dovuto sia dovuto e non soddisfatto» |
| 6 | §5 Deny | "any data other than test data, masked data included" | "masked data included" can attach to the data blocked or to the test data | Read as data blocked, consistent with "only on test data": «ogni dato diverso dai dati di prova, compresi i dati mascherati» |
| 7 | §5 Rules against under-escalation | "E6 · Measurement", "E6 · Calibration" | Two rules carry the same identifier | «E6 · Misura», «E6 · Taratura» |
| 8 | §6 K8 | "Such cases MUST be flagged as high divergence" | "Such cases" refers to the cases of K7, across the list item | «Questi casi DEVONO essere segnalati come divergenza elevata» |
| 9 | §7 Digital Seniority | "SD level" | The abbreviation SD is never expanded in English; it comes from the Italian "Seniority Digitale" | «livello SD» |

| 11 | §8 Question round | "The answers not asked MUST be taken as correct" | Elliptic: it means the supposed answers to the questions not sent to the Human Manager | «Le risposte alle domande non chieste DEVONO essere prese per corrette» |
| 12 | §8.1 | "Proof of concept and MVP"; "a mock-up (MOC)" | The title names the MVP, which the text does not treat; MOC is an unusual abbreviation | «Proof of concept e MVP»; «un mock-up (MOC)» |

| 15 | §14.1 Level 1 · Evidence | "detection of a deliberately vulnerable test suite" | Literally the suite is detected, not the vulnerabilities placed in it (specification 0.3: "rilevazione di una suite di vulnerabilità inserite di proposito") | Literal: «rilevazione di una suite di prova deliberatamente vulnerabile» |
| 16 | §14.2 Running above the qualified level | "the exception below for the work on test data does not apply to it" | "it" can be the doubt or the case of a class in doubt; "below" points to the next sentence | «l'eccezione indicata sotto per il lavoro su dati di prova non si applica a questo caso» |
| 17 | §14.2 Running above the qualified level | "moving up follows Moving up" | Self-reference to the paragraph of the same name | «la salita segue Salita» |
| 18 | §16 Version history | "Version 1.0 supersedes the Italian specification 0.3" | The document is 1.0.0-rc.7, a release candidate not yet in force | «La versione 1.0 sostituisce la specifica italiana 0.3» |
| 19 | §2 Why Eiraman exists | "this knowledge is, by default, information"; "in work and organisational contexts it is blocking by default" | §14 names the modes "informative" and "blocking" and sets the blocking mode "in every other case", doubt included; here the wording is looser ("information", "work and organisational contexts") | «questa conoscenza è, per impostazione predefinita, informazione»; «nei contesti di lavoro e organizzativi è bloccante per impostazione predefinita» |
| 20 | §15 Measuring the method | "the Maintainers (§4) for the Extended Framework and, with them, the Role Profiles and Tools" | "them" has no clear antecedent (the Maintainers, or the Extended Framework, which is singular); "Maintainers" is capitalised here and not elsewhere | «i Manutentori (§4) per il Framework esteso e, con essi, i Role Profile e gli Strumenti» |
| 21 | §15 Measuring the method | "they MUST record as a fault, that is, any failure of the delivery to do what its instructions say or what was asked, everything that does not work" | The definition of fault is inserted between the verb and its object | Same order: «DEVONO registrare come Fault, cioè ogni caso in cui la consegna non fa ciò che le sue istruzioni dicono o ciò che è stato chiesto, tutto ciò che non funziona» |
| 22 | §15 Measuring the method | "whoever coordinates the work on the method MUST propose to the Human Manager" | The role is not among those of §4, and "the Human Manager" of the method is not defined in the Guide | «chi coordina il lavoro sul metodo DEVE proporre al Manager umano» |
| 23 | §15 Measuring the method | "for a class of project"; "the classes the bench represents" | "class" can be the project classes 0 to 2 of §14 or a kind of project | «per una classe di progetto»; «le classi che il banco rappresenta» |
| 24 | §15 Measuring the method | "An outcome of "not demonstrated" is enough for neither" | "neither" refers to the two consequences of the two sentences before it (reducing or removing a rule; stating the baseline for a class) | «Un esito «non dimostrato» non basta né per l'una né per l'altra cosa» |
| 25 | §15 Measuring the method | "MUST be recorded before the campaign and MUST NOT change after it" | "it" can be the campaign or the recording of the hypotheses | «DEVONO essere registrate prima della campagna e NON DEVONO cambiare dopo di essa» |
| 26 | §2 The laws of the AI Manager | "MUST act by four laws"; "The task is completed in the best way that its context allows" | The four laws are statements in the indicative, not commands; the obligation is in the sentence that introduces them | Indicative kept: «Il compito è completato nel miglior modo che il suo contesto consente» |
| 27 | §2 The laws of the AI Manager | "a partial remedy that returns costs more" | "returns" is elliptic: the problem comes back, not the remedy | «un rimedio parziale che si ripresenta costa di più» |
| 28 | §4 AI Manager | "the Human Manager's time and accountability (§4) need to know later who decided what" | Time and accountability are the subject of "need to know"; the reference to §4 is inside §4 | «il tempo e l'accountability del Manager umano (§4) richiedono di sapere in seguito chi ha deciso che cosa» |
| 29 | §4 AI Manager | "estimates in numbers the cost of each alternative, spending, context and the Human Manager's time" | The three items can be the components of the cost or further things to estimate | Same apposition: «il costo di ciascuna alternativa, spesa, contesto e tempo del Manager umano» |
| 30 | §4 Approvals in the Human Manager's stead; §9 Manager View | "the Human Manager's decisions with their words"; "the Human Manager's decisions in their words" | "their" can be the Human Manager or the decisions | «le decisioni del Manager umano con le sue parole» |
| 31 | §4 Approvals in the Human Manager's stead | "when the outcome of the judge of Discovery is not «convergent», that is «convergent with reservations» or «not convergent»" | "that is" lists the outcomes other than «convergent», one of which is named «not convergent» like the condition | «non è «convergente», cioè è «convergente con riserve» o «non convergente»» |
| 32 | §4 Approvals in the Human Manager's stead; §5 Do through the AI Manager | "Other decisions, one-way doors included, it decides itself within the delegation of the mandate" | §4 Human Manager ("MUST decide Do escalations") and §5 ("Silence never approves a Do", "The irreversible option MUST NOT be executed without an answer") are not reworded for the decisions that the AI Manager takes in the Human Manager's stead. Resolved (CR-041, WP-41A2), source unchanged: no contradiction. The §4 item decides Do escalations «under the same condition», directly or through the AI Manager; under §5 Do through the AI Manager (D-041-E) the AI Manager's decision is the answer to the Do; Silence never approves a Do covers a Do that the AI Manager brings to the Human Manager, and silence still approves nothing | «Le altre decisioni, comprese le porte a una via, le decide da sé entro la delega del mandato»; §5 unchanged |
| 33 | §4 Derogations of the AI Manager | "it does not lift the spending cap or the security chosen by the Human Manager" | "lift" can mean to raise or to remove, and fits a cap better than "the security chosen" | «non toglie il tetto di spesa né la sicurezza scelta dal Manager umano» |
| 34 | §4 Derogations of the AI Manager | "a wrong outcome of a security control is repeated rather than left out" | Literally the outcome is repeated; the control is meant | Literal: «un esito sbagliato di un controllo di sicurezza viene ripetuto anziché tralasciato» |
| 35 | §7.1 Promotion and reduction | "The mandate sets the share of runs per cycle that model promotions reach" | "reach" does not say whether the share is a ceiling or a target; the earlier text was a limit ("MUST NOT exceed"). Resolved (CR-041, WP-41A2), source unchanged: the descriptive form follows D-041-H, recorded in RFC 0008 (key words) and in the CHANGELOG of 1.0.0-rc.7 under Removed, which calls the share a cap, a value of the mandate and context of the first law. The MUST NOT was not in 1.0.0-rc.6: it entered with RFC 0007 (commit `0b4146e`) and was never released | «la quota di run per ciclo che le promozioni di modello raggiungono» |
| 36 | §8 Discovery group | "no role of the group is required, and the number can be below five" | The list that follows names five roles and, where present, the architects: it is not said what "five" counts. Resolved (CR-041, WP-41A2), source unchanged: not contradictory; "five" is the minimum of five roles of 1.0.0-rc.6, removed (CHANGELOG of 1.0.0-rc.7, Removed) | «nessun ruolo del gruppo è obbligatorio, e il numero può essere inferiore a cinque» |
| 37 | §8 Iterations; Convergence; §8.1 Iterate | "one round of the members of the group"; "iterate, in the iterations that the AI Manager opens (Iterations), until consensus within the maximum number of rounds" | One round per iteration against iterating until consensus; "round" also names the rounds of divergence. Resolved (CR-041, WP-41A2), source unchanged: not contradictory; §8.1 Iterate states that each iteration has one round and that another opens when the AI Manager decides, within the maximum number of rounds; Divergence has its own maximum | «un giro dei membri del gruppo»; «iterare, nelle iterazioni che il Manager AI apre (Iterazioni), fino al consenso entro il numero massimo di giri» |
| 38 | §8 Convergence | "The Human Manager MUST then approve the Vision or relaunch the exploration; when the outcome of the judge is «convergent», Discovery goes on and the AI Manager takes this decision in their stead" | The obligation on the Human Manager and its exception are in the same sentence. Resolved (CR-041, WP-41A2), source unchanged: not contradictory; an obligation with its stated exception, as in the §4 item "directly or, where Approvals in the Human Manager's stead allows, through the AI Manager" (D-041-D) | Same order: «Il Manager umano DEVE poi approvare la Visione oppure rilanciare l'esplorazione; quando l'esito del giudice è «convergente», la Scoperta prosegue e il Manager AI prende questa decisione in sua vece» |
| 39 | §14 Dialogue with the Human Manager | "under the first and second laws it does not alter the content" | The earlier text was a prohibition (MUST NOT); the sentence is now descriptive, as every sentence on the AI Manager outside §2. Resolved (CR-041, WP-41A2), source unchanged: the descriptive form follows D-041-H, recorded in RFC 0008 (key words) and in the CHANGELOG of 1.0.0-rc.7 under Removed; the limit is a consequence of the first and second laws, and the attachment of the original risk profile still binds the Tools (MUST) | Descriptive: «per la prima e la seconda legge non altera il contenuto» |
